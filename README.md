## What is Qrate?
Qrate is an AI-powered healthcare navigation platform designed for urban LGBTQ+ individuals, sexually active women, and asexual/aromantic individuals in India who face discrimination, judgment, or denial of care when accessing health services.

Finding a doctor who won't misgender you, pathologise your sexuality, or morally police your choices should not require insider knowledge or community word-of-mouth. **Qrate makes safety a searchable, verifiable attribute of a healthcare provider.**

## The Problem
- LGBTQ+ individuals in India routinely delay or avoid medical care due to fear of discrimination
- Sexually active unmarried women face moral policing from gynaecologists
- Asexual and aromantic individuals are frequently pathologised by mental health professionals
- The burden of safety assessment falls entirely on the patient — often requiring them to "out" themselves in high-stakes clinical settings

## Core Features
- **🔍 Provider Discovery Engine**: AI-powered search and recommendation matching users to verified queer-affirmative doctors, therapists, gynaecologists, and mental health professionals — filtered by location, specialty, and certification status. Built on a low-disclosure principle.
- **📅 Direct Booking System**: In-platform appointment scheduling that eliminates awkward phone calls.
- **🎓 Provider Certification Module**: Training pathway → public badge → community verification.

## Datasets
1. **Community spreadsheets** — crowdsourced safe provider lists
2. **Structured reviews** — queer-affirming parameters  
3. **Certification data** — training completion + verification

*All with consent, anonymised, community-governed.*
