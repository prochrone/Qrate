Qrate 🏥🌈
A feminist AI platform connecting LGBTQ+ individuals and sexually active women in India with queer-affirmative healthcare providers.

What is Qrate?
Qrate is an AI-powered healthcare navigation platform designed for urban LGBTQ+ individuals, sexually active women, and asexual/aromantic individuals in India who face discrimination, judgment, or denial of care when accessing health services.

Finding a doctor who won't misgender you, pathologise your sexuality, or morally police your choices should not require insider knowledge or community word-of-mouth. Qrate makes safety a searchable, verifiable attribute of a healthcare provider.

The Problem
LGBTQ+ individuals in India routinely delay or avoid medical care due to fear of discrimination

Sexually active unmarried women face moral policing from gynaecologists

Asexual and aromantic individuals are frequently pathologised by mental health professionals

The burden of safety assessment falls entirely on the patient — often requiring them to "out" themselves in high-stakes clinical settings

Core Features
1. 🔍 Provider Discovery Engine
AI-powered search and recommendation matching users to verified queer-affirmative doctors, therapists, gynaecologists, and mental health professionals — filtered by location, specialty, and certification status. Built on a low-disclosure principle: users can find safe care without revealing sensitive identity information.

2. 📅 Direct Booking System
In-platform appointment scheduling that eliminates the need for phone calls or in-person visits before first contact — a known barrier for queer patients who fear discrimination during initial outreach.

3. 🎓 Provider Certification Module
A structured training pathway covering queer and sexuality-inclusive care standards. Providers who complete the curriculum earn a Qrate Certification badge displayed publicly on their profile — creating accountability and a verifiable trust signal for patients.

Datasets
Qrate is built on three data streams:

Community-generated crowdsourced spreadsheets — LGBTQ+ communities across India have independently compiled lists of safe and affirming providers by city and specialty. These grassroots datasets form Qrate's seed directory.

Structured community reviews — Submissions from users rating providers on queer-affirming practice parameters (pronoun use, judgment-free communication, STI literacy, trans-affirming care).

Provider self-declaration data — Linked to certification training completion and governed by community verification protocols.

All data is collected with explicit informed consent, anonymised where appropriate, and governed by a community advisory panel.

Feminist Design Principles
Qrate is built on feminist AI ethics:

Data minimisation — users never required to disclose identity to receive recommendations

Community accountability — provider badges verified by community reviews, not self-declaration

Intersectionality — parameters co-designed with trans women, asexual individuals, and gender-nonconforming people

Algorithmic transparency — all recommendation logic documented and open-sourced

Open licensing — MIT licence for all models; CC BY 4.0 for anonymised datasets
